# Geometric composition mode

This is an additional visual branch, not a replacement for cartoon figures. A figure may mix flat faces, lightly shadowed planes, thin stacks and a few deeper modules. Do not force every component into a thick cuboid.

## Shape vocabulary

Choose constructions that help express existing content:
- Offset/overlapping rectangles, ordered thin planes and segmented bars.
- Parallel stacks, deliberately opened fans, tiled matrices and stepped arrays.
- Paired trapezoids with a central rectangle for a coherent folded/expanded assembly.
- Shallow prisms, open/partial nested frames, circles, rings and sector combinations.
- Flat labels and quiet panels alongside stronger focal modules.

Counts and arrangements are paper-specific. A fan is not a cycle; a broad arrow is not a measured larger quantity. Apply consistent local perspective where that construction calls for it. Do not add scientific modules merely to display more shapes.

## Color, light and weight

Use a hierarchy of pale region fills, medium module faces and a few deeper focal accents. Keep semantic color assignments consistent within a figure. Coordinate front/top/side tones, line weight, saturation, depth and shadow direction. Mix light planes with heavier objects. Shadows and gradients can vary by visual role without making every element equally prominent.

Explore soft and stronger-contrast palettes when requested. Shape diversity must be visible in silhouette and construction; a palette swap or thicker outlines alone is not a structural variation. Make composition fuller with useful groupings, feature arrays, nested detail and better scale, while reserving routing/label space. Avoid gratuitous clutter.

## Designed arrows

Possible treatments include outlined/filled arrows, softly shaded ribbons, gradual taper, curved bundles, rounded elbows, branches/merges and dashed secondary paths. Assign line styles and weights consistently by relationship. Record tail/head, direction, branch count and relative placement. Check for extra reverse arrowheads and branches that join the wrong component.

## Assets and PPT

Generate one coherent module per transparent PNG when its parts move together; split independently positioned components. Generate designed arrows separately when independent placement is useful. Keep text, equations, dimension labels and legend wording out of artwork, reserving clear overlay areas.

Default PPT mode is `text-placement`: native text/formulas plus empty slots and useful placement/anchor information. Compound shape/arrow/frame assets stay outside the PPT for manual assembly. The original cartoon native-structure mode remains available.

## Construction contract and review

For each asset, describe the source region, semantic role, important count, intended parallel/fan relation, expected front/side faces and complete contour. Use the two-axis review in [visual-review.md](visual-review.md). Similar colors do not establish fidelity. A reference with malformed faces is not authority to repeat those faces.

Future style branches should supply their own visual vocabulary and asset/native split while reusing selection state, scientific constraints and both review gates.
